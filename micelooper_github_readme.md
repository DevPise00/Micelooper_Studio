# 🎸 MiceLooper Studio Pro
**Turn any cheap 2.4 GHz wireless mouse into a professional, zero-latency guitar loop pedal.**

![MiceLooper Studio Pro](https://via.placeholder.com/800x400.png?text=MiceLooper+Studio+Pro+-+Dark+Theme+UI)

MiceLooper Studio Pro is a Windows-based DSP audio engine that lets musicians record, overdub, and mix parallel guitar loops hands-free. Instead of buying a $150+ hardware loop station, MiceLooper uses raw hardware isolation to turn a standard $5 wireless PC mouse into your foot pedal—without interfering with your normal laptop cursor!

---

## 🚀 What It Does
* **Hardware Isolation:** Pairs exclusively with your floor mouse. Your foot taps the mouse to record/play/overdub, while your main trackpad/mouse remains 100% free for normal computer use.
* **Low-Latency DSP:** Built on PortAudio for real-time, zero-latency looping.
* **Interactive UI:** A beautiful, dark-themed virtual stage where a dynamic spotlight follows your loops.
* **Parallel Tracks:** Record multiple independent loops (Rhythm, Lead, Bass, Percussion) and mute/unmute them individually on the fly.

## 🔑 How to Install & Activate
1. Download `MiceLooper_Setup_v1.0.exe` from the **Releases** tab on the right side of this GitHub page.
2. Run the installer and launch the app.
3. If you purchased a key, click the **⋮** menu in the top left and select **Settings & Audio I/O**.
4. Paste your 19-character key (e.g., `MICE-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`) into the PRO LICENSE KEY box and click **ACTIVATE PRO**.
5. Connect your guitar, drop a wireless mouse on the floor, and start looping!
